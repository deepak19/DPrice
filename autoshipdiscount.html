    Business Rules

    • Guests: guests get no FTAD/RAD.
    • FTAD, default: an eligible SKU gets FTAD once per user.
    • FTAD, Brand scope (FTADScope = BRAND): FTAD is once per user per Brand. Every eligible SKU of that Brand in the same order gets FTAD. After checkout, no SKU of that Brand gets FTAD again.
    • No Brand: a product with no xp.Brand uses the default values and SKU scope.
    • Brand specific FTAD, RAD, MaxLimit can be optionally configured in sitecore.
    • MaxLimit is only meant for FTAD.
    • Fallback: FTAD %, RAD % and MaxLimit each fall back to the default on their own when the Brand value is empty in sitecore.
    • MaxLimit: it caps the total FTAD of one Brand in one order. Products with no Brand form one group that uses the default cap. A MaxLimit of 0 means no cap. The order's FTAD is the sum of the capped groups.
    • Over the cap: every line in the Brand is scaled by cap / total, and the rounded amounts add up exactly to the cap.
    • RAD: it applies only on recurring orders, to flagged lines, at the Brand RAD or the default.
    • No backfill: history starts empty at release.
    • Cancel or Substitution: cancelling an order or substituting a product before shipping should reset product's/brand's specific history.
